    Questions people ask
    
      
    Short answers to the things most clients want to know before we start.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        How long does a project take?
        
    A launch page takes about a week, a business site about three weeks, and a web app six to ten weeks. The estimator above gives a timeline for your exact choices.
    
      
    
      
    
        How does payment work?
        
    Half to start the work and half when the site goes live. Larger apps can be split into milestones. Everything is agreed in writing before I begin.
    
      
    
      
    
        What do I need to give you?
        
    Your logo and brand colours if you have them, the text and photos you want on the site, and a few websites you like. If you do not have the text yet, I can write it for you as an extra.
    
      
    
      
    
        Will it work on phones?
        
    Yes. Every page is designed on a phone screen first and then scaled up to tablets and computers.
    
      
    
      
    
        Do you offer support after launch?
        
    Business sites include one month of support. For any other project you can add three months of support in the estimator. After that, fixes and changes are quoted as small separate jobs.
    
      
    
      
    
        Who owns the website?
        
    You do. The code, design and content are handed over to you once the final payment is made.
    
      
    
      
    
        Can you fix a site someone else built?
        
    Usually, yes. I start with a short review of what is slow, broken or insecure, and then give you a fixed price to repair it.
    
      
    
      
    
        What happens to the details I send?
        
    The contact form does not store anything. It opens WhatsApp or your email app with the message ready, so what you write goes only to me.
