How to leak less

  1. Use a browser with fingerprint protection. Firefox with strict tracking protection and Brave both reduce what sites can read.
  2. Install fewer extensions. Each one makes your browser a little more unusual and easier to recognise.
  3. Block third-party trackers. A content blocker such as uBlock Origin stops most fingerprinting scripts from loading at all.
  4. Check site permissions. Review camera, microphone, location and notifications in your browser settings and remove what you do not use.
  5. Keep work and personal browsing apart. Separate browser profiles stop one identity from following you into the other.
